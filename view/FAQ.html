Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your stay.

Standard check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability.

Yes, a complimentary continental breakfast is served every morning from 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM at our main restaurant.

Yes, guests have complimentary access to our rooftop infinity pool and 24-hour fitness center.

Cancellations made 24 hours before the check-in date are free of charge. Cancellations made later than that will incur a one-night fee.

Yes, we offer airport transfers. Please contact our concierge at least 24 hours prior to your arrival to schedule a pickup.

We are a pet-friendly hotel. However, a small cleaning fee applies, and pets must not be left unattended in the rooms.

Yes, we provide free valet parking for all our guests in our secure underground garage.

Yes, room service is available 24/7. You can order food and beverages directly from your room telephone.

Yes, extra beds or cribs can be provided upon request for an additional charge, subject to room size availability.

All our rooms are non-smoking. However, we have designated smoking areas located outside the main lobby and on the terrace.
